Creator & Voice

Sasha D. Mirkovich

Саша Д. Мирковић

Sasha created Lorestage Gaming – Игроказ as a reflection of a childhood ambition: exploring the stories, ideas, and philosophies hidden within video games. Holding master's degrees in Sociology and Computer Science, he works as a Data Analyst / Engineer, bringing together analytical thinking and a passion for narrative. Some of his favourite games include S.T.A.L.K.E.R. trilogy, Mass Effect trilogy, Star Wars KOTOR series, Medieval II: Total War, Crash Bandicoot 3, and MediEvil, while his favorite console remains the original PlayStation.
